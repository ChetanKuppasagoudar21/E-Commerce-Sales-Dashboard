# Sales-Dashboard
📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI
An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes e-commerce sales performance across categories, customer segments, products, regions, and time.
Built to showcase business insights, KPI tracking, and data storytelling.

🚀 Live Highlights
💰 $2.3M in total sales
📦 5,009 total orders
📈 $286K total profit
🔄 6% return rate
📊 Sales & profit growth tracked YoY (2011–2014)

🎯 Business Questions Answered
Which categories and products generate the most revenue?
Which customer segment contributes the highest sales?
How do sales and profits trend over time?
Which categories have higher return rates?
How does performance vary by region?

📈 Key Insights
Technology is the highest-revenue category ($0.84M)
Consumer segment contributes ~50% of total sales
Staples is the best-selling product
Office Supplies have the highest return volume
Sales and profits show consistent YoY growth

🧩 Dashboard Features
KPI Cards (Sales, Profit, Orders, Quantity, Returns)
Sales by Category (Bar Chart)
Sales by Customer Segment (Donut Chart)
Top 5 Best-Selling Products
Returned Orders by Category
Monthly Sales & Profit Trends (YoY)
Interactive Region Slicer
Geographical Sales Map (USA)
